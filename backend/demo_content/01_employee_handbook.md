# Acme Software Inc. — Employee Handbook

*This handbook is fictional demo content written for a portfolio project and
does not describe a real company.*

## Welcome

Acme Software Inc. is a 140-person software company headquartered in
Austin, Texas, with a fully distributed engineering team. We build Beacon,
a project and task management tool for small and mid-sized teams. This
handbook covers the policies every Acme employee is expected to know.

## Work Hours and Remote Work

Acme operates on a flexible-hours model. Employees are expected to be
available for a four-hour "core overlap" window, 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM in
their local time zone, so that meetings can be scheduled without excessive
back-and-forth across time zones. Outside of core hours, employees may
structure their day as they choose, provided their deliverables are met.

Remote work is the default arrangement at Acme. Employees may work from
any location within their country of employment without prior approval.
Working from a different country for more than 30 consecutive days
requires written approval from People Operations, due to tax and
employment-law considerations.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar
year, credited at a rate of 1.25 days per completed month of employment.
PTO may be used for vacation, personal time, or minor illness; separate
sick leave is described below.

Unused PTO may roll over into the following year, up to a maximum of 5
days. Any unused PTO beyond that 5-day cap is forfeited at year end and is
not paid out, except where required by local law.

To request PTO, submit a request through the HR portal at least 5 business
days in advance for requests of 3 days or longer. Requests of 1–2 days may
be submitted with 48 hours' notice. Your manager must approve the request
before it is considered confirmed.

Separately from PTO, employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do
not roll over and are intended for the employee's own illness or a medical
appointment.

## Benefits

Acme offers the following benefits to full-time employees, effective on
the first day of employment:

- Health insurance with 100% of the employee premium covered by Acme, and
  70% of dependent premiums covered.
- A 401(k) retirement plan with a 4% company match, vested immediately.
- $1,000 per year in professional development funds, usable for courses,
  books, or conference attendance, subject to manager approval.
- 12 weeks of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver, and 4 weeks
  for the secondary caregiver, following the birth, adoption, or fostering
  of a child.

## Code of Conduct

Acme employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners
with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are
prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination. Employees who experience or witness a violation should report
it to People Operations or through the anonymous ethics hotline listed on
the internal HR portal.

Conflicts of interest — such as a financial stake in a vendor or
competitor — must be disclosed to your manager and People Operations in
writing before engaging in the related work.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses (travel, client meals, approved software subscriptions)
are reimbursed through the expense portal. Submit expense reports within 30
days of the expense being incurred; reports submitted after 90 days will
not be reimbursed except in unusual circumstances approved by Finance.
Expenses over $500 require pre-approval from your manager before they are
incurred, not just at reimbursement time.
