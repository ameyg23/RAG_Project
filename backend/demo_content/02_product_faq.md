# Beacon Product FAQ

*Beacon is a fictional product invented for this demo knowledge base.*

## What is Beacon?

Beacon is a project and task management tool built for small and
mid-sized teams. It combines boards, lists, and a lightweight timeline
view, so a team can track work the way that suits them without switching
tools. Beacon is built and maintained by Acme Software Inc.

## What pricing tiers does Beacon offer?

Beacon is offered in four tiers, billed either monthly or annually (annual
billing gets a 20% discount):

- **Free** — up to 3 team members, 2 active projects, 1 GB of file
  storage. No credit card required.
- **Team** — $8 per user per month. Unlimited projects, 10 GB of file
  storage per workspace, and access to the timeline view.
- **Business** — $15 per user per month. Everything in Team, plus custom
  fields, advanced permissions, and priority support with a 4-hour
  response SLA.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing. Everything in Business, plus single
  sign-on (SSO), an audit log, and a dedicated customer success manager.

## What integrations does Beacon support?

Beacon integrates natively with Slack, GitHub, Google Calendar, and
Zapier. The Slack integration posts task updates to a channel of your
choice and lets you create a Beacon task directly from a Slack message.
The GitHub integration links pull requests to Beacon tasks and can
automatically move a task to "Done" when its linked pull request merges.
The Zapier integration allows connecting Beacon to over 3,000 other
apps for custom automations. Additional integrations are tracked on
Beacon's public roadmap page.

## Can I export my data out of Beacon?

Yes. Every tier, including Free, can export all project data as a CSV
file at any time from the workspace settings page. Business and
Enterprise tiers additionally support a full JSON export that preserves
task relationships, comments, and attachment references, which is useful
for migrating to another tool or for an internal data archive.

## Is Beacon secure and compliant?

Beacon encrypts data at rest using AES-256 and encrypts all data in
transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Acme Software Inc. completed a SOC 2 Type
II audit, and the report is available to Business and Enterprise
customers under NDA upon request. Enterprise customers can additionally
request a signed Data Processing Addendum (DPA) for GDPR compliance.

## What support does Beacon offer?

Free and Team tier customers can reach support by email, with a target
response time of 2 business days. Business tier customers get priority
email support with a 4-hour response SLA during business hours (9 AM–6 PM
in the customer's primary time zone, Monday–Friday). Enterprise customers
are assigned a dedicated customer success manager and have access to a
shared Slack channel with the Acme support team.

## Does Beacon have a mobile app?

Yes, Beacon has native iOS and Android apps that support viewing and
updating tasks, receiving push notifications for mentions and due dates,
and offline viewing of recently synced boards. The mobile apps do not
currently support the timeline view, which is web-only as of this
writing.
