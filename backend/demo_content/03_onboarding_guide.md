# New Employee Onboarding Guide — Acme Software Inc.

*Fictional demo content: a new-hire onboarding guide for the fictional
Acme Software Inc.*

## Before Your First Day

People Operations will email you an offer confirmation and a link to the
onboarding portal about one week before your start date. Through that
portal you will:

- Complete your I-9 (or local equivalent) employment eligibility
  paperwork.
- Choose your equipment: a company laptop (14-inch or 16-inch model) and
  any peripherals you'd like shipped to your home address.
- Set up direct deposit for payroll.

Your laptop and any requested peripherals are shipped to arrive 2–3
business days before your start date. If anything hasn't arrived by the
day before you start, contact IT at it-support@acme-demo.example
immediately.

## Your First Day

Your manager will send a calendar invite for a 30-minute welcome call at
9:00 AM your local time on your first day. Before that call:

1. Log into your new laptop using the temporary credentials emailed to you
   the night before.
2. Set up multi-factor authentication on your Acme account — this is
   required before you can access any internal systems (see the
   Information Security Policy for details).
3. Join the `#new-hires` Slack channel, where People Operations posts a
   short welcome message and introduces you to the team.

Your first day also includes a 1-hour session with a People Operations
representative covering benefits enrollment, and a 1-hour session with
your manager to review your first-week goals.

## Your First Week

During your first week, new hires typically attend:

- A 2-hour "Acme 101" session covering company history, how Beacon is
  organized as a product, and who the founding team is.
- A 1-hour engineering (or department-specific) onboarding session run by
  a senior teammate, covering internal tools and how work is tracked in
  Beacon itself — Acme famously "dogfoods" its own product.
- A 30-minute one-on-one with your manager at the end of the week to
  check in on how onboarding is going and answer any questions.

New hires are not expected to ship meaningful work in their first week.
The first two weeks are explicitly reserved for ramp-up, reading
documentation, and pairing with teammates.

## Who to Contact

| Topic | Contact |
|---|---|
| Equipment, laptop, account access | IT Support (`it-support@acme-demo.example`) |
| Benefits enrollment, payroll questions | People Operations (`people-ops@acme-demo.example`) |
| Your day-to-day work, goals, feedback | Your manager |
| Expense reports, reimbursements | Finance (`finance@acme-demo.example`) |
| Reporting a workplace concern | People Operations, or the anonymous ethics hotline on the HR portal |

## Probation Period

New hires are on a 90-day introductory period. This is not a trial in the
sense of reduced benefits — full benefits begin on day one — but it is a
period during which your manager will check in more frequently (biweekly
one-on-ones instead of monthly) to make sure you're set up for success. At
the end of the 90 days, your manager completes a short review and
confirms your transition to standard employment status.
